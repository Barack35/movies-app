## 4. Ready-to-Paste Video Setup (CapCut / Canva)

### Set up (both apps)
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (TikTok/Reels/Stories) or 16:9 (YouTube/WhatsApp)
- **Length:** 30 seconds total
- **Music:** pick a fast/energetic trending beat; start it at full volume, fade out in the last 3s
- **Caption style:** bold, white text on a red or black bar so it reads like Netflix branding over movie posters

### Captions to paste (with timing)

**0:00–0:03**
> Tired of paying for movies?

**0:03–0:08**
> Meet Ckflix — unlimited movies & TV.

**0:08–0:14**
> Watch anywhere. No subscription. No credit card.

**0:14–0:20**
> All your favorites — right in your browser, for free.

**0:20–0:25**
> Save your favorites. Drop your opinion. Stream for free.

**0:25–0:30**
> Ckflix. The free cinema that never closes.
> Watch now → barack35.github.io/movies-app

### Step-by-step (CapCut)
1. Open CapCut → **New project** → set ratio to 9:16.
2. **Add clips:** search "movie", "cinema", "popcorn", "TV screen" in the stock library (or import your screen recordings). Drag clips onto the timeline and trim so the 6 scenes roughly match the timings above.
3. **Add text:** tap **Text → Add text**, paste each caption above. Each text should last ~5 seconds, centered, lower-middle of screen. Use a red background bar for the Netflix look.
4. **Transitions:** add a quick **smooth/glitch** transition between scenes (0.3–0.5s).
5. **Add music:** tap **Audio → Music** and pick an upbeat track; trim to 30s.
6. **End card:** on the last 5s, add the Ckflix logo (red wordmark) + the URL in big text.
7. **Export:** 1080p, 30fps. Done.

### Step-by-step (Canva)
1. Go to canva.com → **Create a design** → search **"9:16 video"** or **"Reels"**.
2. Search **"movie"** in the video tab; drag 6–8 short clips into the canvas.
3. Add text boxes and paste the captions above; adjust each to ~5s. Use a black background with red text to mirror Netflix's branding.
4. Add a **pop/glitch** transition from the effects menu.
5. **Elements → Audio** to add music.
6. Add the Ckflix logo (red) + URL as the final frame.
7. **Share → Download → MP4**.
